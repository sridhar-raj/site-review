# Rubric

Score each area 1-5 against these anchors. Give the score that matches the worst thing you saw in that area,
not the average: one unreadable headline makes Clarity a 2 however good the rest is.

## The premium bar

What the work that was approved shares (OriginKit X Post section 01; the Lowfield hero), and what rejected
work lacked:
- **One focal point per screen, big:** a hero object or product moment taking 40-60 % of the first screen.
- **The product working,** not a stock photo, an abstract blob or a screenshot in a tilted frame.
- **Real, specific words and numbers** (a date, a price, a name, a measured result).
- **Decisive type:** one display line with deliberate size and tracking; everything else small and quiet.
- **One palette, one accent,** taken from the brand. Light that looks designed (a key light, a spill, a soft
  shadow), not a flat field.
- Dark cinematic or light editorial both qualify; what fails is generic.

## The six areas

**Clarity**
- 5: the headline says what it is in plain words, key noun early; one sentence says who it's for; one obvious
  action. A stranger gets it in 5 seconds.
- 3: understandable after reading the subhead or scrolling; the action competes with others.
- 1: a slogan that could belong to anyone ("Build the future"); no clear action, or several equal ones.

**Look**
- 5: would sit next to the bar; you couldn't swap the logo for a competitor's; one memorable moment.
- 3: clean and competent but forgettable; a template made tidy.
- 1: template tells everywhere (below), stock imagery, clashing colours, default fonts.

**Hierarchy & layout**
- 5: one focal point per screen; a clear reading order; consistent spacing rhythm; alignment holds.
- 3: mostly ordered, a few sections cramped or same-weight; some misalignment.
- 1: everything shouts; walls of cards; no rhythm; elements collide.

**Mobile** (390 px)
- 5: headline ≤ 4 lines, CTA on the first screen, taps ≥ 44 px, text ≥ 14 px, nothing sideways, the visual
  cropped on purpose.
- 3: works but cramped; CTA below the fold; a few small targets.
- 1: sideways scroll, overlapping elements, unreadable text, the desktop layout squashed.

**Speed & stability** (from the script; unthrottled from this machine, so treat as a floor)
- 5: LCP ≤ 2.5 s, CLS ≤ 0.1, page ≤ 2.5 MB, no errors or broken images.
- 3: LCP 2.5-4 s or CLS ≤ 0.25 or a heavy page; minor errors.
- 1: LCP > 4 s, visible layout jumps, broken images, a popup in front of everything.

**Trust & copy**
- 5: specific claims with numbers, real customers or a checkable spec near the CTA; copy reads like a person.
- 3: some specifics, some filler; generic testimonials.
- 1: filler words ("unlock", "seamless", "supercharge", "next-generation", "the future of"), invented-looking
  stats, logos nobody knows in grey.

## Template tells

Each is fine once, with a reason; together they read as AI-made or templated:
a centred headline with one gradient word; a purple-to-blue glow; an eyebrow pill above the headline; a filled
+ ghost button pair; a grey logo strip; three equal icon cards; "01 / 02 / 03" step labels; a dashboard
screenshot in a tilted frame; glassy cards on a blob gradient; every section the same height and layout;
headlines that start with "Introducing" or "Meet".

## Hero checklist (for a revamp, with `--hero`)

1. Would it sit next to X Post 01 or Lowfield without looking weaker?
2. Is the hero object 40-60 % of the screen and clearly the product working?
3. Cover the logo: could a competitor use this hero unchanged? (Must be no.)
4. Does it read in 5 seconds: what it is, for whom, what to click?
5. Real words and numbers on the object, nothing invented or placeholder?
6. One palette, one accent, none of the template tells above?
7. A different composition, palette and components from the last revamps (the revamp's `history.md`)?
8. At 390 px: headline ≤ 4 lines, CTA on the first screen, the object still visible?
9. Something answers the pointer, eased, and it is calm with no pointer?
